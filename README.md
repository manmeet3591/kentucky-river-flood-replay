# Kentucky River Flood Replay

A replay of the April and February 2025 Kentucky River floods using NASA's OPERA satellite water maps (DSWx-S1) and the USGS gauges at Locks 4, 12 and 14 and on the North Fork at Jackson. It shows what the satellites caught, what they missed, and what a multi-satellite system could add for lock-and-dam inspections and short-range outlooks.

**Live page:** https://manmeet3591.github.io/kentucky-river-flood-replay/

This is a research replay of past floods, not a forecast or warning. For current river conditions and forecasts, use the National Weather Service (https://water.noaa.gov) and the USGS (https://waterdata.usgs.gov).

## Data

- NASA/JPL OPERA DSWx-S1 v1.0 surface water maps (doi:10.5067/OPDSWS1-L3V1), which contain modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2025)
- USGS instantaneous gage height: 03287500 (Kentucky River at Lock 4, Frankfort), 03282000 (Lock 14, Heidelberg), 03282120 (Lock 12 near Irvine) and 03280000 (North Fork at Jackson; provisional and subject to revision)
- National Weather Service flood categories
- US county boundaries
